Module delete_plan 

Source
Expand description

Dry-run of a cascaded removal, executable in batches.

DeletePlan walks the foreign keys (on_delete = "cascade" | "set_null" | "restrict") and the business rules (on_remove_rule) from a root entity without modifying anything. The plan can be inspected (graph, counts, restrictions) and then executed leaves first, in multiple transactions, so the queue lock can be released between batches and the progress reported.

Every step is idempotent and re-validated when executed: a step is skipped when its entity is gone or when any link of its foreign key chain up to the root no longer holds. Rows created after the plan are still cascaded when their parent is removed.
